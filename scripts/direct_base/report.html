Replay protocol

Inputs and scope. Each run uses complete one-minute BingX USDT perpetual OHLCV candles over at most 72 hours. Candidate orders come from the native x02 directional Sets listed in the configuration explorer. Every Set owns its cross-symbol history and has at most one open source position per symbol. Each alternative is an independent paper portfolio of equal base notionals. Simultaneous alternatives are never added together as a tradable portfolio.

Qualification. The 400 native Base paths use Long 10–50 in tens, Short 4–16 in fours and Last 1–5. Each signed Short and Last combination has its own lane. Their three blocks are disjoint, with the newest Last first, then Short, then Long. The native combined PF and Long risk checks also apply. A further 23 standalone Base controls test each individual signed block. Each end sees only the closed members of its exact passing Base lane within the -hour lookback. The 16 independent end gates cover Short 8+, 8−, 12+, 12−, 16+, 20+ and both signs of Last 1–5. The additional 60 two-block end gates require both the newest Last and the immediately preceding Short to pass. Every gate requires all N samples. A negative sign compares gross losses to gross gains at the specified floor and retains the original direction and settlement of the next order.

Executions and costs. Each archive begins with a warm-up of hours to build qualification history. The scored execution book starts flat. The simulation fixes the requested PF floors and turns off automatic hold selection and extra post-win pauses for every alternative. Orders enter at the Base candidate’s actual signal-bar close and receive the native Set’s SL, TP, trailing, cooldown and time exit. A close at the entry timestamp is unavailable to the gate. End gates and Axis can use strictly earlier warm-up closes. Block starts fresh with scored entries. One modeled round trip costs % of notional, using the x02 paper fee policy and slippage allowance. Funding, lot rounding, liquidity, margin and capital limits are outside this replay. Unsettled positions are counted in the open column. Only realized cash enters PF, profitable hours and drawdown.

Axis and Block. Axis tests the exact end-qualified Set’s unweighted history. Pause 1 or 2 skips entries after that many consecutive losses. Prev 4 uses the native 8- and 50-close PF test against 85% of the end floor. Block groups the lane’s own executed entries by entry order in rounds of 1 or 2, with increments of 0.10 or 0.20 base size. A losing round raises sizing and a second losing round can raise it once more. The increase lasts until the accumulated weighted deficit recovers. A round completes after all its members close. The largest tested multiple is 1.4×. The setup compares all 20 Axis × Block combinations and a separate no-end-gate control.

Reading the results. Execution PF divides realized gains by absolute realized losses after sizing and costs. ∞ denotes a positive, loss-free sample and — an undefined PF. A profitable hour requires strictly positive aggregate net over the UTC hour of settlement. All clock hours count in the primary percentage. The explorer also reports the active-hour percentage. Drawdown groups simultaneous settlements and measures realized equity from zero. Identical candidate histories can yield identical statistics at different thresholds, especially for Last 1. Ranking millions of alternatives creates selection risk even with a separate holdout.
